Who We Are

A Legacy of
Global Trade

MadhavaKrishna International LLP is a premier export-import house headquartered in Hyderabad, India. We serve as the trusted bridge between India's abundant produce and global markets, operating with integrity, precision, and deep market knowledge.

Founded with a vision to bring the finest of India to the world, we have grown into a trusted partner for buyers and sellers across six continents — navigating regulations, logistics, and cultural nuances with equal finesse.
